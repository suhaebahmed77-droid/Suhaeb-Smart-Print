How to Print on Your Epson L3250

Why slots start Offline: Browsers cannot detect physical Wi-Fi or USB printers automatically. That prevents false "Online" indicators and fake "Printed" statuses.
1

Enable Epson Print Enabler on Android

Install Epson Print Enabler from Google Play, then go to Phone Settings → Connections → Printing and turn it ON. Keep phone and Epson L3250 on the same Wi-Fi.

2

Open Customer Batch & Print

In the Admin Queue, tap Preview & Print on a customer's batch, then tap Print This File or Print All Files to open Android's print dialog and pick your Epson L3250.

3

Tap "Mark Printed" After Physical Output

Once the pages come out of the printer, tap Mark Printed. The customer's screen immediately unlocks the UPI QR and Cash payment options.
